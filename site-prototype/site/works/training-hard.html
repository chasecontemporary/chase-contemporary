Training Hard,
by José Dias Herrera depicts Pelé jumping during a Santos FC training session. Presented in a Santos FC tracksuit, Pelé is captured in a moment that reflects the discipline and athleticism behind his legendary career. José Dias Herrera, is a Brazilian photographer renowned for documenting Pelé and some of football’s defining moments. From the 1950s through the 1970s, Herrera accompanied Pelé as he rose from promising young athlete to global icon.
