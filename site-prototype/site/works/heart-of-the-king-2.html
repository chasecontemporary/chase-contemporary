Pelé
Heart of The King
30 x 40 in
40 x 60 in
This classic 1976 image by Luiz Paulo Machado captures the celebrated moment when sweat on Pelé’s shirt formed a heart across his chest. Widely published and instantly recognizable, the hand-signed photograph held deep personal meaning for Pelé, who called it “a photo only God can explain,” and remains a powerful symbol of his legacy of peace, love, and goodwill.
