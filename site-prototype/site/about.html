THE PROGRAM CENTERS ON CONTEMPORARY AND STREET ART — RICHARD HAMBLETON, RETNA, KENNY SCHARF — ALONGSIDE THE ARTISTS THE GALLERY HAS DISCOVERED AND CHAMPIONED, INCLUDING ANDRES VALENCIA.
