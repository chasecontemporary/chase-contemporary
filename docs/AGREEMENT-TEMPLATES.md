# Agreement templates

What the engine prints today, what has to be confirmed before a collector ever signs one, and
who answers each question. Generated by `crm/lib/agreementsPdf.js` in the invoice brand system
(Nimbus, 64pt margins, tracked caps, wall-label works, stacked logo, page footer).

**Status: unreviewed.** While `AGREEMENTS_REVIEWED` is anything but `1`, every page carries a
grey diagonal DRAFT, PENDING COUNSEL REVIEW watermark and the footer line "This template has
not yet been reviewed by counsel." Set the variable in Vercel and redeploy the day counsel
clears them, and not before.

**Bernie signature: drop a transparent PNG at crm/assets/img/signature.png and redeploy.**
It prints on the certificate of authenticity above the AUTHORIZED SIGNATURE line at about
150pt wide, with "Bernie Chase, Owner" under the line. With no file there, the line stays
blank for a wet signature. Nothing else changes.

## Purchase agreement

`buildPurchaseAgreement({ invoice, lines, collector, works, sale })`. Multi-page US Letter,
numbered by the invoice number, dated the invoice issue date. It accompanies the invoice, it
does not replace it.

1. **Parties.** Seller: Zenzeba Group Inc, doing business as Chase Contemporary,
   info@chasecontemporary.com. Buyer: name, billing address, email and phone from the
   collector record.
2. **The work or works.** One wall label per work: artist in tracked caps, title in italic,
   year, medium, dimensions, edition, inventory number, with the inventory image.
3. **Price and payment.** Every invoice line (work, service, shipping, tax, credit), the total,
   the deposit due on signing and the balance with its due date, and the wire block from
   `WIRE_INSTRUCTIONS` when that is set. Bank charges are for the Buyer.
4. **Title and risk.** Title passes only on receipt of the full price in cleared funds. Until
   then the Buyer holds the work in trust and may not sell or pledge it. Risk passes on
   delivery to the Buyer or the Buyer's carrier; the Seller insures until then.
5. **Delivery and inspection.** Seller arranges packing, crating and shipping at the Buyer's
   cost unless the invoice says otherwise; nothing is released before payment in full; the
   Buyer reports damage in writing within the inspection window.
6. **Sales tax.** As stated on the invoice; use tax is the Buyer's where the Seller does not
   collect.
7. **Authenticity and title.** Authenticity warranted, good title and the right to sell
   warranted, free of lien or claim, certificate of authenticity delivered with the work, no
   other warranty.
8. **Returns.** All sales final except as required by law.
9. **Default.** Cancel and keep the deposit as liquidated damages, or hold the Buyer to the
   purchase with late interest, or resell and recover the shortfall. Delivery may be suspended.
10. **Governing law.** New York law, courts of New York County.
11. **General.** Entire agreement with the invoice, amendments in writing, counterparts and
    electronic signature valid, severability.

Signatures: BUYER and SELLER (Chase Contemporary, by Bernie Chase, Owner) side by side, each
with a signature line, a printed name line and a date line, always together on one page.

**Deliberately not in here:** there is no artist consignment split and no resale royalty
clause. The gallery owns its inventory outright and sells its own property. If that ever stops
being true, consignment paper is a different template, not a clause bolted onto this one.

## On approval agreement

`buildApprovalAgreement({ hold, collector, artwork, opts })`, numbered by the inventory number.

1. Parties, as above.
2. The work: wall label plus image.
3. Approval period: out date to the return-by date (the hold's expiry), extendable in writing.
4. Price during the approval period: the artwork price, or `opts.price_cents` when a special
   price was agreed, held for the Buyer until the return date.
5. Custody and care: held in trust, climate controlled private residence, no cleaning,
   conservation, framing, alteration, lending, exhibition or moving without written consent;
   only the Seller or an approved carrier handles the work.
6. Insurance: the Buyer insures for the full price from delivery until return, all risks,
   Seller named as loss payee, evidence on request.
7. Return or purchase: return by the date at the Buyer's cost in the original packing, or
   elect to purchase and an invoice issues. Neither by the date and the Seller may collect at
   the Buyer's cost or treat it as a purchase.
8. Title stays with the Seller throughout; risk sits with the Buyer from delivery to return.
9. Governing law, as above. 10. General, as above. Then the same signature blocks.

Note for the floor: BRAND.md records that the gallery does not run take-home approvals today,
so the approval surfaces stay quiet until Bernie says otherwise. The paper exists so that the
day one goes out it goes out covered.

## Constants to confirm

All five live at the top of `crm/lib/agreementsPdf.js`, each marked "confirm with counsel".

| Constant | Prints as | Confirm with |
|---|---|---|
| `GOVERNING_LAW.law` | the laws of the State of New York, without regard to its conflict of laws rules | Counsel |
| `GOVERNING_LAW.venue` | the state and federal courts sitting in New York County, New York | Counsel |
| `LATE_INTEREST` | 1% per month on an overdue balance | Counsel, and Kristine on what she actually charges |
| `INSPECTION_DAYS` | 5 days from delivery to report damage | Counsel, and Jeremy-side shipping practice |
| `DEFAULT_DUE_DAYS` | balance due 7 days after issue when the invoice carries no due date | Kristine |
| Deposit forfeiture | any deposit kept as liquidated damages on default | Counsel. Liquidated damages clauses get read closely in New York; the number has to be a genuine pre-estimate of loss, not a penalty |

## Questions for counsel

1. Is the deposit forfeiture enforceable as drafted, or does it need a stated percentage cap?
2. New York sales tax: is the use tax sentence enough where the gallery does not collect, and
   does the gallery need a resale or exemption certificate on file for trade buyers?
3. Does the authenticity warranty need a stated survival period, and is a right of rescission
   on a proven authenticity failure expected at these price points?
4. Is the "all sales final" clause safe next to any New York consumer right of cancellation,
   given some sales close off premises at fairs?
5. Electronic signature through DocuSign: anything the gallery must do to make the executed
   copy stand up, beyond keeping the certificate of completion?
6. Does the on approval agreement need a UCC filing or any bailment language beyond "in trust"
   to protect the gallery if a collector's estate or creditors reach the work?
