## Gallery Overview

- **Founded:** 2017 by Bernard "Bernie" Chase, co-founder of Symbolic Motors (San Diego exotic-car dealership); some promotional articles also call him "former owner of Symbolic Motor Company and RM Auctions" (US Times Now / The American Reporter — both read as placed/sponsored pieces). Chase's fuller backstory (classic cars, jewelry, a 1980s federal sentence, managing Ronnie Wood's art career) is in his own words in the July 2023 Artnet News interview by Kenny Schachter (source #2).
- **Location history (all sourced below):**
  - 2017: opens at **231 10th Avenue, Chelsea, NYC** (Workhouse PR; Yelp listing now marked CLOSED)
  - 2018: second Chelsea space at **521 West 23rd Street** (Workhouse PR; Yelp)
  - 2021 (May/July): **66 Newtown Lane, East Hampton, NY 11937** (27east; Waze/MutualArt listings; US Times Now)
  - ~late 2021–2022: consolidates into **413–415 West Broadway, SoHo** — a 10,000 sq ft flagship between Spring & Prince (Forbes, Artforum artguide, Art Miami dealer page). Artsy snippets credit Ole Aakjær's "Alter Ego" with opening the SoHo space; Forbes/Incollect describe the Hambleton retrospective (spring 2022) as inaugurating the just-opened 10,000 sq ft gallery.
  - 2024: "Chase Contemporary gallery Palm Beach" announced on Instagram (post C6eyONRORSH, ~May 2024). **Street address not found — UNVERIFIED.**
  - Los Angeles: the gallery's marketing boilerplate ("locations in New York City, Palm Beach and Los Angeles") appears on Facebook/Artsy/LinkedIn. One search snippet gave **900 N Broadway #1090, Los Angeles, CA 90012** (Chinatown/Blossom Plaza) — **UNVERIFIED**, no retrieved page confirms it.
- **Current status (verified by direct fetch, Aug 2026):** chasecontemporary.com is now a **Shopify e-commerce storefront**. Its own About page says the gallery ran the SoHo flagship where it "hosted more than 50 solo exhibitions," and has since "transformed into a digital-first platform" with **no permanent gallery space**, doing pop-ups and fairs (it names Art Miami, Art Palm Beach, Art New York, Dallas Art Fair). Current featured offering: an exclusive **Pelé collection** of hand-signed silver gelatin prints. No physical address or phone is published on the current site — contact is info@chasecontemporary.com only.
- **Program:** blue-chip secondary market (Peter Beard, Yayoi Kusama, Keith Haring, Warhol-era) + emerging/mid-career artists, with a heavy **street-art emphasis** (Richard Hambleton, Kenny Scharf, Retna, RISK, LA II). Known for in-gallery artist residencies (artists working in the basement during their shows — ABC7, Artnet interview) and for launching child prodigy Andres Valencia.
- **Fairs (sourced):** Art New York 2019 (booth ANY306, Chuck Hoberman kinetic works), Art Miami (incl. 2021 — Valencia's sold-out booth — and 2025 — Peter Tunney), Palm Beach Modern + Contemporary 2022. Bernie Chase told Schachter in 2023 he deliberately avoids Frieze/Basel/Armory.
- **Wikipedia:** an English Wikipedia article "Chase Contemporary" exists on offline mirrors (wikipedia.nucleos.com) but en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chase_Contemporary now returns 404 — apparently deleted. **UNVERIFIED as to why.**
