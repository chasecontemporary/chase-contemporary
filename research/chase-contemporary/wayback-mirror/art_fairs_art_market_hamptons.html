Art Market Hamptons

Bridgehampton Art Fair

Thu, Aug 12, 2021 - Sun, Aug 15, 2021

Back

Market Art + Design, the East End's premier art fair, returns to The Bridgehampton Museum in August of 2021. Market Art + Design's eleventh edition will feature 80 top galleries presenting the best in modern and contemporary art and design in an expanded, museum-quality pavilion.

Market Art & Design, with its lively atmosphere, is enjoyed as a meeting place for collectors, artists, and dealers as well as a cultural day out for the simply curious. This art fair is a mix of modern and contemporary art curated by more than 75 international galleries. Alongside the exciting works of art is a section dedicated to Design featuring furniture and objects.

Since its creation in 2010, the art fair has grown year after year and attracts around 15,000 visitors worldwide.

Market Art & Design takes place every summer in the Hamptons, at the Bridgehampton Museum, under vast marquees to make the most of the summer weather. Market Art & Design is part of an important group that also held Texas Contemporary, Art on Paper NY, Seattle Art Fair, and Art Market San Francisco.
