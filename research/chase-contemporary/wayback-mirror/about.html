Chase Contemporary is a contemporary art gallery established in 2017 with two prominent locations in the heart of Chelsea, New York City’s fine art district. The gallery exhibits work in a variety of media including painting, photography, sculpture, and works on paper. Chase’s core program is to exhibit both blue chip art as well as work by emerging and mid-level career artists.

Chase Contemporary has an in-house printer and has published prints for a number of gallery artists including Ole Aakjaer and Tom Everhart, as well as publishing catalogs and artist books. The gallery regularly hosts artist talks and lectures, events, and benefit auctions, and participates yearly in major art fairs.
