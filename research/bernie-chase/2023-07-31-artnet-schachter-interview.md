## Key biography facts (all from this interview)

- Age 65 at publication (July 2023) → born ~1957–58. Grew up Gardena → San Fernando Valley → Venice, CA. Father was a CPA.
- Operates "a few contemporary art galleries on West Broadway" in SoHo, NYC — mostly street-art vein. Artists dealt per Schachter: Keith Haring, Kenny Scharf, Richard Hambleton, R. Crumb, S. Clay Wilson, Robert Williams.
- Does NOT exhibit at Frieze/Basel/Armory; Schachter calls him "among the least pretentious, most honest art dealers who ever dealt art."
- Career arc: bicycles/VW rebuilds → Jeeps → Mercedes → Ferraris. Vintage Motorcar Companies (storefront, '86–'91) → **Symbolic Motors** (founded 1990 with brother **Mark Chase**, La Jolla; profiled in Forbes 1998 "Would you buy a car from these brothers?"; clients incl. Rob Walton, Sir Michael Kadoorie, Ron Burkle). Partner in **RM Auctions** pre-Sotheby's. Later **Symbolic & Chase** jewelry, Bond Street, London.
- Sold/restored a Ferrari 275GTB/4 NART Spider; sold cars to John Moores (San Diego Padres owner). Philanthropy push at Scripps Research (~$60M raise for neglected/childhood disease research, per the Forbes piece).
- Brunei chapter (1990s): consignment liquidator for Prince Jefri Bolkiah's assets (Patek Caliber 89s, colored diamonds, jeweled carpets), based in Geneva, sold via Antiquorum; introduced Joe Hage and Ron Burkle to each other.
- Polo chapter (~2013): sponsored team at Guards, Prince Charles played as team captain; sat in the Queen's box, escorted the Queen to present the Queen's Cup; Symbolic beat Hermès with Prince Harry playing.
- Candid past (all on the record in this piece): drug use from ~age 10–11; federal marijuana conviction at 21 (stipulated facts, 5-year sentence reduced to ~3 served: MDC, Lompoc, Terminal Island; refused to inform); rehab ~25 yrs before the interview, diagnosed bipolar; calls jail "the best thing happening in my life... it gave me my work ethic."
- R. Crumb: bought a ~600-piece Crumb collection (traded his Ed "Big Daddy" Roth body of work for it), sold partly to Mark Parker (Nike CEO); flew Crumb out to authenticate the whole collection.
- Old Masters chapter: bought from Richard Green (London) on his signature "receipts + 15% over cost" deal; Lucas Cranach the Elder dispute resolved amicably; had a 30-year above-hammer/clawback arrangement with the auction houses ("106%... 2% to my lawyer").
- Art-gallery entry: partner in **Scream Gallery**, London (owned by the Ronnie Wood family; friends with Jamie and Tyrone Wood); managed Ronnie Wood's art career, produced shows worldwide, traveled with the Rolling Stones for years.
- Chase gallery timeline: first NYC space in **Chelsea ~2014** ("nine years ago"), up to three Chelsea spaces; a Texas company bought out the leases mid-pandemic; opened **Chase Contemporary in SoHo (West Broadway, between Prince and Spring)** ~early 2022 ("about a year and a half" before July 2023). Doing artist studios + fashion, modeled on the '80s Chelsea studio-artist scene.
