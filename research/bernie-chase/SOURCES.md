## Synthesized bio

**Bernard "Bernie" Chase** (b. ~1958; age 65 at his July 2023 Artnet interview) is an American entrepreneur, collector, and art dealer, and the founder of **Chase Contemporary**, founded **2017** in Chelsea, New York; later a 10,000 sq ft flagship at **413 West Broadway, SoHo**, plus **East Hampton (2020)**, **Palm Beach (spring 2023)**, and **Los Angeles**.

- **Early life:** Grew up in Gardena and the San Fernando Valley, CA; son of an accountant. *(Artnet/Schachter)*
- **Prison (early 1980s):** Convicted ~age 21 on federal drug charges; served ~3 of a 5-year sentence (MDC, Lompoc, Terminal Island). "Jail was probably the best thing happening in my life." *(Artnet/Schachter)* A 2014 San Diego Reader piece citing court records describes a Mar 18, 1981 cocaine-distribution charge plus a firearms count, 3-year sentence, $25,000 fine — **conflicts with the interview's marijuana framing**.
- **Cars:** Vintage Motorcar Companies (1986–91) → co-founded **Symbolic Motor Car Company**, La Jolla, with brother **Marc Chase** (garage-founded ~1985 per Forbes; "1990" per the interview) — ~$120M/yr, one of the world's largest collectible-car dealers; Forbes profiles 1998 & 1999; clients incl. Rob Walton, Ron Burkle, the McCaws, Otis Chandler; helped raise ~$60M for Scripps Research's Institute for Childhood and Neglected Diseases. Claim of "former owner of RM Auctions" appears only in syndicated PR — **UNVERIFIED, likely inaccurate** (interview says "partner with RM Auctions").
- **Jewelry/luxury:** Post-prison Houston jewelry/pawn circuit trade; later liquidated Prince Jefri Bolkiah (Brunei) assets — Patek Caliber 89s, colored diamonds — from Geneva via Antiquorum; **Symbolic & Chase** jewelry, Bond Street. Polo team captained by Prince Charles at Guards; socialized with Queen Elizabeth II. *(His own account; not independently corroborated)*
- **Art entry:** Partner in London's **Scream Gallery** (Ronnie Wood family, via son Tyrone); managed **Ronnie Wood's** art career; co-curator of Wood's April 2012 SoHo show "Faces, Time and Places" (Getty/Alamy captions, credited "Symbolic Collection"); Symbolic London staged a Wood retrospective in 2012.
- **Peter Beard litigation:** Sued by Peter Beard (filed 2015) over three works ("The Snows of Kilimanjaro," "765 Elephants," "Paradise Lost") Chase claimed via a 2013 handshake deal; courts ruled for Beard (Feb 2017; affirmed 2018). Court records also describe Chase as an investor in HG Contemporary (Hoerle-Guggenheim).
- **Chase Contemporary (2017–):** Chelsea (231 10th Ave & 521 W 23rd St) → SoHo flagship; inaugural SoHo show was a major **Richard Hambleton** retrospective (2022). Shows/roster: Hambleton, RETNA, Kenny Scharf, Peter Beard, Carole Feuerman, Imani Bilál, Sky Kim, Bernie Taupin, Connor Brothers; Kusama/Warhol secondary market.
- **Andres Valencia:** Discovered the child prodigy (a family friend's son) at 6–7 — "Andres is going to be an important artist" (relayed via Valencia's father); arranged his record-setting Art Miami 2021 solo booth ($1.3M sales, youngest artist ever); first NYC solo at Chase Contemporary June 2022 (35 paintings, $50k–$125k each).
- **Later evolution:** Nov 2023 — reported merger with UK group **Castle Fine Art** to launch "Castle Contemporary" at 413 West Broadway *(Pretty Renegade snippet; partially UNVERIFIED — ASK BERNIE about current status)*. 2025–26: gallery's own About page describes a "digital-first platform" with pop-ups and fairs; Chelsea marked closed on Yelp; en.wikipedia "Chase Contemporary" article appears deleted (mirror snippet corroborates 2017 founding).
- Signature self-descriptions: "I've always liked art. When I was young, I always had fantasies of being a treasure hunter." / "I make decisions pretty quick—not always the best decisions, but I make decisions."
